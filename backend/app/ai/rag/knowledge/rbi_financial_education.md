## 1. Fundamentals of Individual Financial Prudence

Financial prudence begins with disciplined cash management. Individuals must maintain a clear distinction between inflows (salary, business income, investments) and outflows (mandatory fixed obligations and discretionary spending).

### 1.1 Maintenance of Liquid Reserves
The Reserve Bank encourages all account holders to establish a dedicated emergency reserve prior to undertaking capital market investments. 
- A baseline contingency fund should cover a minimum of 3 to 6 months of mandatory living expenses (rent, utilities, groceries, school fees, and existing loan servicing commitments).
- Liquid reserves must be retained in low-risk, highly accessible financial instruments such as savings bank accounts, sweep-in fixed deposits, or overnight liquid mutual funds.
- Emergency funds should never be exposed to market volatility, illiquid real estate, or equity instruments.

### 1.2 Credit Discipline and Debt Servicing
- **Debt-to-Income Ratio (DTI)**: Total monthly debt servicing commitments (EMIs for home loans, personal loans, vehicle loans, and credit cards) should ideally not exceed 40% to 50% of net take-home monthly income.
- **Credit Card Repayments**: Credit cards offer a revolving credit facility with high annualized interest rates (often exceeding 36% to 42% per annum). Cardholders should always settle the total outstanding balance on or before the due date rather than paying only the Minimum Amount Due (MAD).
- Failure to clear full dues triggers interest on all future transactions from the date of purchase, eroding household savings.

## 2. Digital Banking Safety and Redressal

### 2.1 Confidentiality of Security Credentials
Under RBI consumer protection mandates, financial institutions will never solicit confidential authentication credentials from customers:
- Never disclose UPI PIN, debit/credit card CVV, net-banking passwords, or One-Time Passwords (OTPs) to any caller, third-party application, or unverified link.
- UPI PIN is entered exclusively for debiting an account to make a payment. Receiving funds into an account via UPI never requires entering a UPI PIN.

### 2.2 Timely Reporting of Unauthorized Transactions
Under RBI's limited liability circular for electronic banking transactions:
- Zero customer liability applies if an unauthorized transaction occurs due to contributory fraud/negligence by the bank.
- Zero customer liability applies if a third-party breach occurs and the customer notifies the bank within 3 working days of receiving transaction alerts.
- Delayed reporting beyond 3 working days increases customer liability depending on the bank's approved board policy.
